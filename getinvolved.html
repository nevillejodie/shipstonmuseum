Society Monthly Meetings

2026 - We are thrilled that meetings of Shipston-on-Stour and District History Society have started again! After a long break of some 30yrs, it was time to restart the Society talks and at the oldest pub in town. On the third Monday of every month (excluding December), we meet at 7:30pm in The Black Horse Inn, Station Road, Shipston-on-Stour CV36 4BT for a short talk about local history followed by discussions on the subject. This is free to Society members and our volunteers. If you'd like further information, attend a monthly meeting as a guest and speak with current members and trustees. Membership Form

Talks 'n Guided Walks

There are Guided Walks and from November to March we have a Winter Talks Programme in The Townsend Hall, Shipston-on-Stour. Please visit Eventbrite for the programme + tickets. Society members and Museum volunteers are welcome to attend free of charge.
